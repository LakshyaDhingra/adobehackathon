# Chameleon: Keep the idea. Change the canvas.

A campaign begins with one carefully composed design. Getting it onto every
screen often means rebuilding that same idea over and over. Simple stretching
loses the typography, the hierarchy, and sometimes the brand itself.

Chameleon takes the original elements and a new canvas and designs their
arrangement again. It recognizes headlines, product art, logos and grouped
buttons from the structure of the source. It measures text using the actual
brand fonts, compares several layout strategies, and chooses a composition
that fits while preserving every word, asset, colour and element identity.

The engine runs locally and offline. Its JSON output remains editable layout
data; its preview uses the real cr-renderer pipeline. The studio makes the
result easy to inspect: compare with the original, choose a layout direction,
review warnings, and export a whole campaign in one step. The Express add-on
can insert the rendered result as a new page at the requested size.

Our difficult 17-element leaderboard sample now keeps its text at 7 pixels or
larger, with no local collision or overflow flags. We test beyond the samples
using generated designs and real-world canvas formats. Those local checks are
engineering evidence, not a prediction of the official judge's score.

The hardest cases remain crowded micro banners: preserving all content does
not guarantee comfortable reading at any possible size. Chameleon makes that
trade-off visible instead of silently discarding the message.

One idea. Every canvas. Still unmistakably your design.
